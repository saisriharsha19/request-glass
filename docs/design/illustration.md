# The everyday file: illustration and interaction

Asset: `public/art/desk-folder-v1.png`, 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG. Created with the built-in image generation tool, then copied into the project. The gaze and eyelids are independent SVG UI layers; the original painting stays still. There are no image-generation runtime calls or external image hosts.

The nearby gaze takes inspiration from [DrKain's cursor-following eyes demo](https://codepen.io/DrKain/pen/VeBJEP). Our implementation uses bounded pupil movement inside the illustration, one scheduled frame per pointer update, and no idle animation loop. Leaving resets the gaze. Touch or keyboard activation gives a short double blink. [Reduced-motion preferences](https://web.dev/articles/prefers-reduced-motion) stop gaze and blink animation. Successful saves and completed reminders retain the separate, finite paper-cut celebration.

Final image prompt (built-in mode):

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original hero illustration for ReturnRadar, a playful but carefully designed receipts, documents and calendar web app. Create a single polished editorial illustration on a genuinely transparent background, square composition, all objects comfortably inside the frame. Subject: an open terracotta document folder holding cream receipts and small sage-green record cards, a chunky little calendar card with a simple check mark (no numbers or words), and a beautifully curved ochre pencil leaning across them. One long receipt curls out of the folder in a loose expressive ribbon. The composition should feel like an illustrator arranged a still life of everyday paperwork with a sense of movement and character. Medium: hand-painted gouache and colored pencil with crisp ink contours, subtle paper grain, slightly imperfect cut edges, lovely overlapping shapes and restrained shallow shadows. Palette matches a warm cream, olive-green and burnt-orange app, with a small muted blue accent. Style: sophisticated independent magazine illustration, inviting and tactile, not corporate vector clipart, not glossy 3D. No faces, no eyes, no robot, no sparkles, no lettering, no quotes, no UI screenshot, no border, no giant gradients, no blurred edges. Make the illustration substantial and detailed enough to reward looking closely, while clean and readable at 360 pixels wide.

Eyes were requested after the painting was created and are implemented in code so they can react. The illustration is present on the dashboard and account screen, including narrow phones. Inputs, text and controls do not scale during the interaction.
