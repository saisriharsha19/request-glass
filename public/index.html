Connect your calendar

Subscribe once to see your upcoming Tuckday reminders in Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar. It keeps working when your devices are offline.

Read-only: edit reminders in Tuckday. Your calendar controls how often updates arrive; they may take several hours. Calendar edits do not sync back.

Sign in to connect

The private link shares reminder titles and dates. Anyone with the link can read them. Receipt files, extracted text and notes are excluded.

Google Calendar
  1. Copy the link above.
  2. Open Google Calendar on a computer. Beside Other calendars, select + → From URL.
  3. Paste the link and choose Add calendar.
Open Google Calendar ↗

Google requires its desktop website for this step. The subscribed calendar then appears on your phone.

Outlook
  1. Copy the link above.
  2. Open Outlook Calendar → Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the link, name it Tuckday and select Import.
Open Outlook Calendar ↗
Apple Calendar / other apps

Choose New Calendar Subscription or Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the private URL.

Loading connected calendars…

Sign in to connect calendars

The link is private to your account. Events are stored for syncing; calendar links are never sent to AI.

Where to find the ICS link

Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar. Choose access, click Publish and copy the ICS link shown in your screenshot.

Google: Calendar settings → select a calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Work accounts may restrict this.

iCloud: Enable Public Calendar sharing, copy its webcal link and use https instead of webcal here.

Links must come from the provider’s calendar service. Sharing permissions control which titles and locations appear.
